## USAA and HomeStory (added Nov 8)

- USAA's old "Real Estate Rewards Network" (run through Realogy/Cartus) ended in 2019. Today USAA offers **USAA Agent Connection, powered by HomeStory**: members are matched with agents in HomeStory's network (2+ years' experience, 20+ homes sold, background check) and get a commission credit at closing, paid out of the agent's commission through a broker-to-broker agreement. Enrollment lasts 18 months; USAA financing isn't required.
- HomeStory runs the same program for Chase ("Chase HomeStory / Agent Express"), SoFi, Fidelity and credit unions. Its own site gets almost no search traffic (brand and login searches): the leads come from the banks' members, not Google.
- Searches with real volume around military buyers: "va realtor" 720/mo (KD 27), "usaa real estate" 210 (KD 0), "military realtor" 170 (KD 15), "veteran realtor" 170 (KD 1), "usaa realtor" 70 (KD 5).
