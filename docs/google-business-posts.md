# Google Business Profile posts: November 2026–January 2027

How to post: Google Business Profile → **Add update** → paste the text → **Add button: Learn more** → paste the link →
add a photo (your own: a listing you sold, the team, or a local spot) → **Post**. One a week. Posts stay up; Google
shows the newest first on your profile in Search and Maps.

Every number names its source (house rule). The links carry `utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp-post`
so Google Analytics can tell these visits apart from the profile's website button.

Fair housing: these talk about homes, prices and places only. Don't add "great schools", "safe", "family-friendly"
or anything about who lives in an area.

## Week 3: When to sell

**Text:**

Is spring really the best time to sell? Two years of Twin Cities sales say yes for price and speed: homes that closed
in June 2026 sold for a median $410,000, about 9% above January's $375,000, and spent 42 days on market against 63.
Winter has the least competition: only 7,216 homes were for sale across the region in December 2025, against 12,411
in September 2026.

Planning a spring sale? Most sellers list from late March through May. Now is the time to plan repairs, photos and
pricing.

Source: Minneapolis Area REALTORS® Local Market Update, October 2024–September 2026 (NorthstarMLS data).

## Week 5 (post by Dec. 1): Bought a home this year? Homestead by Dec. 31

**Text:**

Bought a home in 2026? Apply for homestead with your county by December 31, or you'll miss the homestead rate on your
2027 property taxes. On a $395,000 home, homestead takes $11,000 off the taxable value, and it's required for the
state's property tax refund. You apply once, online in most metro counties.

Every metro county's application link, plus who qualifies, in one place.

Sources: Minnesota Department of Revenue (homestead classification and market value exclusion); each county's
