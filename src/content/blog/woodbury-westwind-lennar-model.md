**Westwind is one of Lennar's newer developments in Woodbury. In the video above, I toured the Itasca model: a little over 3,000 square feet with five bedrooms and four baths, and over 4,000 square feet once the basement is finished.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (May 2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The main floor

- A **front office** with lots of natural light and black hardware accents.
- A **dining room** that fits a table for eight, opening to the living room.
- A **kitchen** with tons of cabinets and a huge center island. This model had the **gourmet kitchen** upgrade with Frigidaire Professional stainless appliances, a gas cooktop and a high-end dishwasher.
- A **main-floor bedroom with a 3/4 bath**, great for guests or a relative who'd rather skip the stairs.
- A **drop-zone closet** off the garage and 9-foot ceilings throughout.

Lennar tends to show models at a price you can actually build, without $100,000 in upgrades. Here, the only upgrades were the appliance package, a laundry sink and the door handles.

## Upstairs

- An **enormous loft**.
- **Four bedrooms**, all with walk-in closets.
- A **shared bath** with a door separating the vanity from the tub and toilet.
- An upstairs **laundry room**.
- An **owner's suite** with a soaking tub, separate shower, dual vanities and a very big closet. My only wish: a door on the toilet room.

## The lower level

The unfinished basement adds about **1,200 to 1,500 square feet**, plumbed for a bathroom, with an egress window for another bedroom and room for a family room and storage.

## What it cost

The Itasca at Westwind was priced around **$700,000** for five bedrooms and four baths.
