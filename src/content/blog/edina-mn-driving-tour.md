**Edina homes were selling between the mid and high $600,000s in about 33 days, even in this market. So what makes Edina the suburb so many people want to move to? Here's my driving tour: the shopping, the parks, 50th & France and more.**

## Why people choose Edina

It comes down to location: about **eight miles from downtown Minneapolis**, with about **53,000 residents**, walkability, and a ton of shopping and dining. People often stay for generations, and when people don't move out, demand stays high. See our [Edina community guide](/communities/edina) for current prices.

## 50th & France

The heart of Edina, with about **175 stores**. Many of the boutiques can't be found anywhere else in the Twin Cities. One of my favorite restaurants, **Mr. Paul's Supper Club**, is right here. It's walkable year-round and going through a renaissance with new construction. Head one direction and you're quickly in Minneapolis; most of the other direction is Edina. There's a mix of rentals and single-family homes, so there's something for most people.

## The Country Club district

One of the most sought-after neighborhoods in the metro, established in **1924** as one of Minnesota's first planned neighborhoods. It's walkable and tree-lined, with French, Tudor and English homes. Homes start around **$800,000** and go into the millions.

Because the district is on the **National Register of Historic Places**, you don't see teardowns here like in other parts of Edina. You can add on, but it has to look period-appropriate. Some see that as a limitation; I see it as an asset, because the character you buy is the character that stays.

## France Avenue south of Highway 62

Edina's main commercial district, near **M Health Fairview Southdale Hospital**, with mixed-use residential living (including 55-plus and senior living) close to dining and shopping.

## Rosland Park and Edina's parks

Edina has **40 parks and over 1,500 acres** of parkland, and the parks connect the neighborhoods. **Rosland Park** has 100-year-old oaks, walking paths, a playground, the aquatic center's water slides and a fishing pier on **Lake Cornelia** for panfish, plus skating in winter. The city keeps its parks updated.

## Southdale Center

The **first enclosed mall in the world**, which makes sense in the frozen north, and the longest-running. It's going through a renaissance, with Kowalski's, Tiffany & Co., Burberry and Gucci, a Life Time with rentable workspace, the Twin Cities' only Cheesecake Factory, a nice movie theater and Dave & Buster's, about 100-plus stores in all. A fun personal note: my wife Brooke's grandfather sold Southdale the first time it ever changed hands.

## Galleria Edina

Opened in 1978 on France Avenue and connected to the Westin (which also has private residences), with boutique shopping like Crate & Barrel, Tumi and Yeti, and restaurants like McCormick & Schmick's and Cove, plus underground parking so your car stays out of the snow.

## Centennial Lakes Park

The crown jewel of Edina's parks: a **24-acre park** woven into a commercial district, with walking trails all around, paddle boats and mini golf in summer, a well-kept skating rink with a warming house in winter, and summer concerts. Grab a bite (Cucumbers is a great salad spot), then take a walk. It's a must-visit.

## Is Edina for you?

Home values in Edina have historically been well protected because people want everything it offers: parks, shopping, dining and a central location.
