**Corcoran and Maple Grove sit right next to each other and cover about the same ground. But Maple Grove has over 2,200 people per square mile, and Corcoran has about 200. One is almost built out; the other is just getting started. After 25 years in this market, I can tell you Corcoran is one of the few places where the early window is still open.**

Details are as of my video above (summer 2026).

## Where it is

Drive west out of Maple Grove and the lots get bigger and the sky opens up. You're still in Hennepin County, about 20 miles from downtown Minneapolis, usually **30 to 35 minutes** in normal traffic. [Maple Grove](/communities/maple-grove)'s Arbor Lakes shopping and dining are a city away, with easy access to **Highway 55** and **I-494**. What buyers can't find in older suburbs anymore, Corcoran still has: space.

## Who's building

Builders don't show up until they believe in a market, and Corcoran has a strong lineup building at the same time: **Lennar, Pulte, M/I Homes, Del Webb, Robert Thomas and JP Brooks**.

- **Lennar's Tavera** master-planned community is permitted for over **550 homes**, bigger than what many suburbs this size ever see.
- **Pulte** has two communities, **Walcott Glen** and **Amberly**.
- **M/I Homes** is selling at **Rush Creek Reserve** on the west edge of Maple Grove, near Highway 55.
- **Del Webb** built **Bellwether**, an active-adult community.
- **United Properties** broke ground on **Amira Corcoran**, a 143-unit 55+ apartment community (studios through three bedrooms, with a club room, fitness and yoga space, golf simulator, pool and trails). Move-ins are expected in summer 2027. Its other Minnesota Amira communities are in Minnetonka, Bloomington, Roseville and Lake Elmo, all older, more built-out suburbs.

Development is moving **east to west**. The east side, closest to Maple Grove, is further along (Rush Creek Reserve, Walcott Glen, Amberly). The west side has more open land and acreage communities like **Heather Meadows** by JP Brooks.

## The growth numbers

- **2020 census:** about **6,200** people.
- **Today:** estimates closer to **9,000 to 9,500**, roughly **50% growth** in five to six years.
- **2050:** the **Metropolitan Council** projects Corcoran will more than triple its 2020 population, to nearly **20,000**. In its *Imagine 2050* plan, the Met Council classifies Corcoran as a "suburban edge" community, expected to take on growth for years. It even revised Corcoran's forecast in mid-2025 because the city was growing faster than expected.

Builders read those plans before they buy land; permits, roads and retail follow. More in [Twin Cities suburbs growth to 2050](/blog/twin-cities-suburbs-growth-2050).

## What homes cost

Prices vary a lot by builder and lot:

- **Townhomes and twin homes:** from the low $400,000s, with some options below that
- **Single-level villas:** mid-$400,000s through the $500,000s
- **New single-family:** usually mid-$500,000s to low $700,000s; higher-end homes reach the high $800,000s and well past $1 million
- **Acreage:** JP Brooks home sites at Heather Meadows are **2+ acres**, with homes starting around $900,000
- **Existing homes:** usually mid-$500,000s to low $600,000s, depending on timing and inventory

Buyers who've made the move say the same thing: more house, more land, and still close to what they use every week.

## The window

Every growing northwest metro suburb has had its moment. Plymouth filled in through the '80s and '90s. Maple Grove grew through the '90s and 2000s to well over 70,000 people and has little buildable land left. Corcoran has about the same footprint, but what's still inside its 36 square miles is different.

Right now you can still pick your lot, get into a master plan before it's half built, and find 2-acre home sites. Heather Meadows has a handful of acreage sites left; Rush Creek Reserve has very few. When those communities sell out, the sites don't come back. Larger lots usually go first. I can't predict prices, but choice is the thing to watch.

## The trade-offs

I'm not going to sugarcoat it:

- **You need a car.** There's no fixed-route public transit, and you'll drive for almost every errand.
- **It's not walkable to dinner yet.** Most dining and shopping is a short drive east in Maple Grove.
- **The commute is longer** than [Plymouth](/communities/plymouth), Golden Valley or Minnetonka, usually about 35 minutes.
- **Retail and amenities are still catching up.**

## Who it fits

If you've been touring Maple Grove homes that feel too small for the price, on smaller lots, with older finishes than you wanted, Corcoran is worth a look. It's one of the few places left in the northwest metro for **new construction on a real lot**, one of the few Twin Cities markets where **2+ acres** is still available, and it has an unusual mix of **55+ options**: Del Webb's Bellwether, Lennar's Tavera villa collection and the incoming Amira.
