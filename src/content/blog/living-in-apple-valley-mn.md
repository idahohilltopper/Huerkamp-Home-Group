**I filmed this one at Alimagnet Park, on the line between Burnsville and Apple Valley, where it feels like you're in a forest. That's how close to nature you can get in Apple Valley. Here's what the city has to offer.**

Figures are from when I made the video above (October 2022). See our [Apple Valley community guide](/communities/apple-valley) for current numbers.

## Quick facts

Apple Valley sits south of Minneapolis and Saint Paul, roughly centered between them, about **20 to 30 minutes** from downtown, with an average commute of about **26 minutes**. Neighbors include Rosemount, Eagan, Burnsville and Lakeville. It had about **55,000** residents in about 21,000 households, with a high ownership rate of almost **80%**, and an average home value right around **$400,000**.

## Things to do

- **The Minnesota Zoo**: lions, tigers and bears (oh my), plus reptiles and much more, and an **amphitheater** hosting everything from country to classical to rock.
- **Apple Valley Aquatic Center**: a splash pad for little ones and a pool for everyone else.

## Parks and lakes

Apple Valley has **51 parks**, with hiking, biking and cross-country ski trails, skating rinks, playgrounds, disc golf, dog-friendly spaces and lots of softball fields (adult leagues included). There are six lakes: **Alimagnet, Cobblestone, Farquar, Keller, Lac Lavon and Long Lake**. Most have homes around them and trails and parks along the shore, so you can fish off a pier, kayak or take the dog for a run.

## Food

Everything from steakhouses to burger joints. **The Farmer & The Fishmonger** is a great steak and seafood spot I've been to a few times, and newer places like a Hawaiian poke bowl spot add variety after a day at the zoo or the park.

## Getting around

Most people drive, since things are spread out in Minnesota. The **Minnesota Valley Transit Authority** has a hub in Apple Valley with free parking, and buses can take you to Eagan, Prior Lake, Minneapolis, Saint Paul and beyond.
