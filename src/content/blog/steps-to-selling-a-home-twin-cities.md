**Have a home to sell in Minneapolis or anywhere in the Twin Cities? Here's the 40,000-foot view of how the selling process works, step by step.**

## 1. The first conversation and market analysis

Start by talking with an agent; it's fine to talk with more than one. We'll ask some questions up front: How would you rate your home's condition on a scale of 1 to 10? Is there anything you plan to fix before listing? Then we prepare a **comparative market analysis (CMA)** to see where the market sits for your home. You can also start with a [free home value estimate](/home-estimate).

## 2. The listing appointment

Next, we walk through your home and go over the analysis together. It's a no-commitment meeting: you get a feel for us, and we get a feel for you. We cover expectations, pricing, our marketing plan and fees, so no stone is left unturned.

## 3. Choose your agent carefully

Once you decide to work together, you sign a **listing agreement** that spells out the price, terms and each side's obligations. Before you sign anything, **do your homework**. Look up the agent or team on Google, Zillow, Facebook and Yelp. In my opinion, an agent without a solid track record of reviews either doesn't have many clients or doesn't have many happy ones. Your home is likely your most valuable asset, so don't leave it in average hands. See [our reviews](/reviews) and [team](/team).

## 4. Market the home and wait for offers

Selling is the reverse of buying: once you're listed, you're waiting for a purchase agreement. Your agent's job is to market the home, bring buyers through and get it in front of other agents.

When the market shifts, it can take longer, and many agents have never worked through a down cycle. I've been through several up and down markets, and here's the lesson: **the answer isn't always a price cut**. Sometimes it just takes time. Look at average days on market for your comparable homes. If similar homes took 60 days to sell, cutting your price at day 15 doesn't make sense. There is a time and place for a reduction, and an experienced agent helps you recognize it. More in [the biggest pricing mistake sellers make](/blog/biggest-mistake-minneapolis-sellers-pricing).

## 5. From purchase agreement to closing

Once an offer comes in, we review every detail and call the buyer's lender to confirm they're pre-approved (unless it's cash). After acceptance, we work through the **inspection** (if the offer is contingent on one), the **appraisal** (if there's financing), title work and the lender's requirements to get to a successful closing.

## 6. Close

These days, sellers can often **pre-sign** closing documents, sometimes up to a couple of weeks early, so you don't have to attend. If you'd like to meet the buyers and hand over the keys, we can usually arrange that too.
