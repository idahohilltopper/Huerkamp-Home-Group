**Thinking about moving to Minneapolis? Here are 10 reasons people love living here, from the lakes to the State Fair.**

## 1. Lakes, lakes and more lakes

We're the Land of 10,000 Lakes (it says so on our license plates), and the real number of lakes over 10 acres is more than 11,800. Up north, roads wind around them. All that water means cabins, cottages, lake homes and a lifetime of memories with friends and family. See our [lake homes page](/lake-homes).

## 2. The Mall of America

The largest mall in the United States, in [Bloomington](/communities/bloomington), is enormous: millions of square feet and hundreds of stores. It even has its own ZIP code. If you love shopping, this is the mecca.

## 3. Summer nights

Summer nights here are pretty much unbelievable: windows open and a fresh breeze coming in. Because we're so far north, it doesn't get dark until about 10 or 10:15 p.m. in June and July.

## 4. Pro sports

We have all four major men's leagues (the Vikings, Twins, Timberwolves and Wild), plus pro women's basketball (the Lynx) and men's soccer (Minnesota United), and plenty of college sports. U.S. Bank Stadium and Target Field are great venues, and Minnesota fans are some of the most dedicated anywhere.

## 5. The music scene

Minnesota is home to Prince and Bob Dylan, and to legendary venues like **First Avenue**, plus festivals from spring through fall. It's a great place for a music lover.

## 6. The outdoors

Mountain biking, boating, downhill and cross-country skiing, and some of the best fishing anywhere. Old railroad lines have become public trails, and parks are everywhere. And then there's "up north": head to Duluth and the North Shore and the landscape changes completely.

## 7. Craft breweries

The Twin Cities have more than 80 craft breweries, from big names like Surly and Lift Bridge to small neighborhood taprooms. Almost every town has one.

## 8. The Minnesota State Fair

Twelve days of fun at the end of summer, nearly 2 million visitors, and just about anything you can imagine on a stick. It's called the Great Minnesota Get-Together (and sometimes the Great Minnesota Sweat-Together, since it's August).

## 9. Homeownership

Minnesota has one of the highest homeownership rates in the country, around 75%.

## 10. Affordable living

Compared with the coasts, homes here cost far less, so you get a lot more for your money. See what that means today on our [Twin Cities market page](/market).
