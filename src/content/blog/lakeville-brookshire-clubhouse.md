**Brookshire is one of Lakeville's newest developments, with resort-style living: three types of single-family homes plus townhomes, all built around a clubhouse that cost over $6 million.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (October 2024). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The clubhouse

- A **gathering room** with a fireplace, a big island, booths, a shuffleboard table and lots of natural light, which residents can reserve for get-togethers.
- A **meeting room** with a smart TV, handy if you work from home.
- A **fitness center** with treadmills, rowers, a Smith machine, dumbbells and kettlebells, plus a **yoga and cycling studio**. It could replace a gym membership.
- A **screened porch** with TVs and fireplaces, outdoor seating, cornhole and bocce.
- A **pool** (closed for the season when I filmed), **tennis courts**, a **basketball court** and a **playground**.
- **EV chargers**.

## A model for about $550,000

- About **2,400 square feet**, overlooking a pond.
- A wide-open kitchen with a big island, sod and smart-home features included.
- A **walkout lower level** with a bedroom, a bath, a family room and deep under-stair storage.
- Upstairs: three bedrooms, a full bath and an **owner's suite** with a walk-in closet, dual vanity and large shower.

## The Jordan model for about $650,000

- About **3,000 finished square feet**, not counting the basement.
- A dining room off the entry, and a spacious kitchen and living room with the gourmet appliance package.
- A **main-floor bedroom with a 3/4 bath**, great for guests or a relative who'd rather skip the stairs.
- A mudroom with storage and a **staircase tucked away** from the front entry.
- Upstairs: a big **loft** with views over the pond to the clubhouse, a laundry room, four bedrooms with walk-in closets, and an owner's suite with a soaking tub, dual vanity, two closets and a private toilet room.
