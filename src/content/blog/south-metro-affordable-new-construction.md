**Meadowview Preserve is Pulte's newer development south of the river, with lots of home sites available when I filmed. In the video above, I toured the Mercer model, about 2,700 square feet with four bedrooms upstairs.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (filmed in December, published April 2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The main floor

- An **inviting entry** with a spot for a small office and a half bath down the hall.
- A **wide-open kitchen** with a massive matte-finish island, a deep black sink centered in the island, white cabinets with black hardware, white counters, double ovens and a cooktop. Pulte's design details, like the light fixtures, really stand out.
- A **walk-in pantry**, a drop zone with a bench off the garage and a nook big enough for a second home office.
- Dining space that seats about eight, and durable **engineered wood floors**.
- A linear fireplace that's **actually gas**, not electric, so it can heat the space if the furnace goes out. Stacked stone and slatted accent walls on each side are a nice touch.

## Upstairs

- An **owner's suite** with the same slatted accent wall, room for a king bed and a sitting area, and ceiling speakers.
- An **owner's bath** with double sinks, a walk-in tiled shower with vertically stacked subway tile, a tile floor (many builders skip it), a semi-private water closet and a closet with a window.
- **Three more bedrooms**, including one with a walk-in closet with a window, and a roomy second bath with double sinks.
- A **big laundry room** with cabinets.

There's not a lot of wasted space; it feels warm and inviting without being oversized. The model also had a deck and a fenced yard to show the lot size.

## The lower level

The full basement has **9-foot ceilings** (many builders use 8) and mechanicals tucked in a corner. It's roughed in for a bathroom, with an egress window spot for a bedroom, plus room for a big family room, a pool table or a wet bar. Finishing it was estimated at about **$40,000 to $45,000**.

## What it cost

The Mercer model was priced around the **mid-$600,000s**.
