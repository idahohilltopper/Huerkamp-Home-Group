**Most people think Bloomington is just a suburb with a big mall. That's not the whole story. It's one of the largest cities in Minnesota, a favorite for buyers who want to be close to Minneapolis without living in it, and east and west Bloomington feel completely different. Here's what it's really like.**

Prices are as of my video above (April 2026). See our [Bloomington community guide](/communities/bloomington) for current numbers.

## What Bloomington is

Bloomington sits in Hennepin County on the north side of the Minnesota River, about **10 miles south of downtown Minneapolis**. I-35W runs right through it, and I-494 runs east to west along the north side. Two things made it famous:

- **The Mall of America**, the largest mall in the U.S. by retail space, with more than 520 stores and restaurants, an indoor theme park and an aquarium, and over 30 million visitors a year.
- **MSP Airport**, right next door: a major Delta hub, repeatedly ranked #1 in North America for passenger satisfaction among mega airports. Many residents can reach the terminal in under 10 minutes.

The I-494 corridor is also one of the region's biggest job centers, with corporate offices, logistics, airport and mall jobs. Think of Bloomington as the **convenience hub** of the Twin Cities.

## It's greener than you'd expect

About **a third of Bloomington is parkland or open space**. Along the Minnesota River on the south side, it immediately feels different: trees, wild turkeys, bald eagles and the **Minnesota Valley National Wildlife Refuge**, with miles of trails. In the west, **Hyland Lake Park Reserve** has trails, open views and disc golf, and next to it, **Hyland Hills** has downhill skiing, cross-country skiing and snowboarding. (Minnesota's ski culture is stronger than people think; Lindsey Vonn got her start at nearby Buck Hill in Burnsville.)

What you won't find is a small historic downtown with a charming main street; some areas are big retail and parking lots.

## West Bloomington vs. East Bloomington

Where you live in Bloomington changes your drive, your budget and the homes you'll see.

- **West Bloomington** has more and larger single-family homes and a more traditional neighborhood feel. The median was around **$390,000**, with many homes in the upper $300,000s to low $400,000s.
- **East Bloomington** is more affordable, with post-war single-family homes, townhomes and low- and high-rise condos, and quick access to retail and freeways. The median was closer to **$325,000**.

Citywide, values sit around the mid-$300,000s, compared with roughly $450,000 to $750,000 in neighboring Edina and Eden Prairie. Neither side is better; drive both and see which fits.

## The housing stock

Bloomington grew fast after World War II, so you'll see ramblers, split-levels and two-stories from the **1950s through the 1970s**, with big yards and mature trees. There are almost no new neighborhoods, just small infill pockets and a few newer townhomes near I-35W and the river bluffs. Many homes have solid bones, but older homes mean older systems, so get the roof, furnace, electrical and plumbing inspected and budget for updates. (See [6 red flags in older homes](/blog/old-minneapolis-homes-red-flags).)

## Pros

- **Location.** Minutes from the airport and the Mall of America, with I-35W and I-494 connecting you to almost anywhere in the metro, and the Blue Line to the airport and downtown.
- **Outdoor space.** The wildlife refuge, Hyland Lake Park Reserve, Hyland Hills and miles of river trails, which is rare this close in.
- **Value.** Strong access to jobs, shopping and the airport for less than Edina, Minnetonka or Eden Prairie.

## Cons

- **I-494 traffic**, especially eastbound, at almost any hour before 10 p.m., plus heavy shopping days near the mall. (The mall's roads and parking were well planned, so it's still easy in and out.) Test your commute before you buy.
- **Some areas feel more practical than charming**, with large retail areas and freeways nearby.

## Who Bloomington fits

- **Frequent travelers:** a 10-minute drive home after a flight changes your routine.
- **People who want metro access** without living downtown.
- **Buyers who want a strong location at a lower price.**
- **Outdoor lovers.**
