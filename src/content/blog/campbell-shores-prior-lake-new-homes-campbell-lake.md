A new neighborhood is coming to the east side of Prior Lake. On Oct. 13, the Prior Lake City Council gave [final approval to Campbell Shores](https://plnewscompass.org/12414/city/campbell-shores-gets-final-pud-plat-approval-from-prior-lake-city-council/), a 62-lot single-family development near the intersection of 170th Street and Marschall Road, according to the Prior Lake News Compass. Utility work and home construction are expected to begin this fall, with model homes expected to open for viewing in spring 2027.

If you've driven Marschall Road (County Road 17) lately, this is the land between the road and Campbell Lake. Here's what has been approved, what's planned around it, and what it means if you're buying or selling in Prior Lake.

## Where it is

The site is about 40 acres, [directly north of the Spring View Meadows neighborhood, west of Marschall Road and east of Campbell Lake](https://plnewscompass.org/12414/city/campbell-shores-gets-final-pud-plat-approval-from-prior-lake-city-council/). The land was farmed before and includes wetlands and wooded areas, and the News Compass reports that significant grading has already taken place.

The developer is **Pinnacle Family Homes**, which the News Compass reported in June [will also be the primary builder](https://plnewscompass.org/?p=9687).

## The homes

When Pinnacle brought the plan to the city, it described custom-built homes [priced from $750,000 to $1 million](https://plnewscompass.org/?p=9687), with full basements, one- or two-story layouts and several walk-out options. The October article notes that this was the price range the company stated at the start, so expect final prices to depend on the lot, the plan and the finishes you choose.

For a sense of where that sits in Prior Lake: the city's median sale price over the 12 months through September 2026 was **$500,000**, up 2.3% from a year earlier, according to the [Minneapolis Area REALTORS® Local Market Update for Prior Lake](https://maar.stats.10kresearch.com/docs/lmu/2026-09/x/PriorLake) (NorthstarMLS data). Campbell Shores is aimed well above the middle of the market.

## Half the land stays open

Campbell Shores was approved as a **shoreland Planned Unit Development (PUD)**. In exchange for flexibility on lot sizes and side-yard setbacks, [50% of the site will be preserved as open space](https://plnewscompass.org/12414/city/campbell-shores-gets-final-pud-plat-approval-from-prior-lake-city-council/) through outlots and conservation easements.

Four of those outlots will be deeded to the City of Prior Lake for open space or park land, and **two of them will serve as trail corridors**, according to both the [June](https://plnewscompass.org/?p=9687) and [October](https://plnewscompass.org/12414/city/campbell-shores-gets-final-pud-plat-approval-from-prior-lake-city-council/) News Compass reports.

## How it got here

- **June 9, 2026:** The City Council approved the preliminary plat and PUD, [the News Compass reported](https://plnewscompass.org/?p=9687). At the time, the city's Community Development Director, Casey McCabe, said that working with Scott County and its Watershed Management Organization early should help streamline the design process.
- **Oct. 13, 2026:** The council gave [final PUD and plat approval](https://plnewscompass.org/12414/city/campbell-shores-gets-final-pud-plat-approval-from-prior-lake-city-council/).
- **This fall:** Utility installation and home construction expected to begin.
- **Spring 2027:** Model homes expected to be open for viewing.

## Changes coming at Marschall Road and 170th Street

The development sits next to an intersection Scott County plans to change. According to the October News Compass report, the county's planned improvements at [County Road 17/Marschall Road and 170th Street East](https://plnewscompass.org/12414/city/campbell-shores-gets-final-pud-plat-approval-from-prior-lake-city-council/) include:

- a future **roundabout**,
- a **pedestrian trail underpass**,
- regional stormwater facilities, and
- new right-of-way designations.

The city's approval requires Pinnacle to coordinate with the county on those improvements, with **no net financial impact on the city**, and to keep appropriate access for Spring View Meadows homeowners while the work goes on. For construction dates on the intersection itself, check [Scott County's road project pages](https://www.scottcountymn.gov/); the county sets that schedule, not the developer.

## What it means if you're buying or selling nearby

- **Buying new construction in Prior Lake?** Model homes are the time to compare. Visit in spring 2027, and bring your own agent on the first visit: builders' sales staff work for the builder. Ask for the lot premium, what's included versus upgraded, the HOA (if any) and what it covers, and how the outlot trails connect.
- **Thinking about a lot that backs up to open space?** With half the site preserved, many lots will border outlots, wetlands or trail corridors. Ask which lots back onto city-owned outlots and what's planned there, because a trail behind your yard is a feature for some buyers and a drawback for others.
- **Living in Spring View Meadows or along Marschall Road?** Expect construction traffic from utility and home building, and later the county's intersection work. The city's approval says access for Spring View Meadows homeowners has to be maintained.
- **Selling a home in Prior Lake?** New homes priced from about $750,000 to $1 million compete most directly with existing homes in that range. Below that, a resale home with a finished lower level and established landscaping is a different product than a new build. In September, Prior Lake sellers received **96.5% of their original list price** and homes took **48 days** on the market until sale, with **3.8 months' supply** of homes for sale ([Minneapolis Area REALTORS®](https://maar.stats.10kresearch.com/docs/lmu/2026-09/x/PriorLake)). Pricing right from day one matters.

## Keep an eye on

- Model home dates and pricing from Pinnacle Family Homes as spring 2027 gets closer.
- Scott County's schedule for the Marschall Road and 170th Street roundabout and trail underpass.
- Which outlots the city takes for parks and trails, and how they connect to existing trails.
