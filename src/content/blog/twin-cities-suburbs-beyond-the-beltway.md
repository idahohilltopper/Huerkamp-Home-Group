**If you're still hunting for your dream home inside the I-494/I-694 loop, you're often fighting over leftovers, paying too much and losing to cash offers. Some of the best places to live in the metro sit just outside that beltway, with new construction, more land for your money, lakes and trails. Here are the best outer-ring suburbs in each direction, with real prices and commutes.**

For context: the Twin Cities metro median hit **$390,000 in 2025** (average over $466,000), with about 2.5 months of supply, still a seller's market. One of the biggest edges a buyer can have is looking where others aren't. Prices below are 2025 figures, as of my video above.

## North

Communities like **Blaine, Andover, Ramsey, Elk River, Rogers and Anoka** form one of the most exciting growth corridors in the state.

- **[Blaine](/communities/blaine)**, on the edge of the beltway, offers great value and the **National Sports Center**, one of the largest amateur sports complexes in the world.
- **[Rogers](/communities/rogers)** got another I-94 off-ramp with the Dayton Parkway interchange (2021), plus a 185,000-square-foot **Cabela's** that draws people from across the region, alongside Cub, Target, gyms and clinics.
- **Elk River** sits on the Mississippi, with river access and bluff views, and a **Northstar commuter rail** station into downtown Minneapolis.

## East

- **[Woodbury](/communities/woodbury)**, the east metro powerhouse: a huge retail and dining scene and an almost ridiculous number of parks and trails. Median about **$410,000** (November 2025). Demand is high, and you're paying for the name.
- **[White Bear Lake](/communities/white-bear-lake)**, one of my favorites: a historic downtown with coffee shops, boutiques and lakeside restaurants, plus water ski shows, polar plunges, art fairs and farmers markets. Median about **$435,000 to $457,000**. It has 24 public parks; Saint Paul is 20 to 30 minutes away, and Minneapolis 35 to 45.
- **[Stillwater](/communities/stillwater)**, the oldest city in Minnesota, on a bluff above the St. Croix National Scenic Riverway, with a historic brick Main Street of wine bars, bookshops, antique stores and restaurants. Entry level about **$420,000 to $500,000**, with river and lakefront much higher.
- **[Cottage Grove](/communities/cottage-grove)**, the east-side value play: median about **$380,000** (October 2025), new construction from the $400,000s from multiple builders, about 25 to 35 minutes to Saint Paul and 35 to 50 to Minneapolis.

## South

- **[Lakeville](/communities/lakeville)**: median about **$514,000** (July 2025), lots of lakes and an easy I-35 commute.
- **[Prior Lake](/communities/prior-lake)**: real lake life, with a median around **$526,000 to $532,000**.
- **[Shakopee](/communities/shakopee)**: more than Valleyfair, with Canterbury Park and Mystic Lake nearby. Median about **$405,000 to $408,000**, and 35 to 50 minutes to Minneapolis via Highway 169.
- **[Farmington](/communities/farmington)** and **[Rosemount](/communities/rosemount)**, two of the most underrated suburbs in the Twin Cities. Farmington (about **$403,000**) is the most affordable, a former farm town that still has some small-town character; Rosemount was about **$436,000**.

## West

- **St. Michael** and **Albertville**, practically twins on I-94 and among the fastest-growing in the metro. St. Michael was about **$430,000 to $470,000**, with Fox Hollow Golf Club, the Ellingson Car Museum and new master-planned neighborhoods; Albertville was about **$390,000 to $440,000**, with the Albertville Premium Outlets.
- **[Waconia](/communities/waconia)**, farther out (45 to 55 minutes to Minneapolis), sits on 3,200-acre Lake Waconia, with a charming downtown, a farmers market, and Schram Vineyards and Sovereign Estate nearby. About **$390,000 to $440,000**.
- **Delano** and **[Hanover](/communities/hanover)**, for buyers who want space and quiet. Delano (about **$380,000 to $430,000**) sits on the Crow River with a sweet small-town downtown; Hanover is even more rural, with new communities on acreage. They're great for remote workers.
- **[Victoria](/communities/victoria)**, the premium pick near Lake Minnetonka without the lake price: about **$500,000 to $580,000**, 30 to 45 minutes to Minneapolis, with charm, trails and a tight-knit feel.

## My ranking of the four quadrants

4. **North**: great new construction value and recreation, but the most commute variability.
3. **West**: incredible lifestyle and scenery, but Highway 12 and 212 commutes can be painful.
2. **South**: Lakeville alone nearly makes it #1, with lakes, amenities and a consistent I-35 commute.
1. **East**, by a hair: Woodbury, White Bear Lake's lifestyle, Stillwater's character, Cottage Grove's value, and two separate routes into both downtowns.

The real answer: the best quadrant is the one that fits your commute, lifestyle and budget. [Ask us anything](/contact), or see [where the metro is growing](/blog/minneapolis-outer-ring-suburbs-growth).
