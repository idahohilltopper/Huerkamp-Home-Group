**Whispering Waters is one of Key Land Homes' newer communities in Shakopee, mostly two-story homes in a wide range of sizes and prices. In the video above, I toured the largest model, which reaches about 4,600 square feet with a finished basement.**

Details are as of my video above (2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The community

Homes at Whispering Waters range from about **2,700 square feet** to about **4,600 square feet** finished. [Shakopee](/communities/shakopee) is one of Minnesota's oldest communities, and today it's full of new construction. **Highway 169** is close by and connects to I-494. MSP Airport is about 20 to 30 minutes away, and downtown Minneapolis is typically under 35. See my [Shakopee driving tour](/blog/shakopee-mn-driving-tour) and [why Shakopee will never be the same](/blog/shakopee-mn-growth-amphitheater).

## The main floor

- A **wide living room** off the entry with a linear fireplace, 9-foot ceilings, lots of recessed lighting and fun wallpaper textures.
- **One of the biggest kitchen islands I've ever seen**, seating five, with a large cooktop and range hood, a hidden dishwasher, a walk-in pantry and a coffee bar with floating shelves.
- An **8-foot sliding door** to the backyard (this lot has an egress lower level).
- A tucked-away half bath and a **drop zone** with hooks and a bench.

Kitchen, dining and living spaces flow together, so it's great for entertaining. It almost feels like a rambler, even though it's a big two-story.

## Upstairs

- A **loft** that works as a second gathering space.
- **Four bedrooms, each with bathroom access**: the owner's suite, a Jack-and-Jill bath between two bedrooms, and a fourth bedroom with its own bath.
- An **owner's bath** with a floor-to-ceiling tiled shower, soaking tub, double vanity and a private water closet with a window.
- A laundry room with a pop of cabinet color and a broom closet, plus a small **office** that's perfect for working from home.

## The lower level

The unfinished basement is huge, with mechanicals tucked in a corner. With one egress window, there's room for at least one more bedroom, a bath and a big rec room. This plan could become **six bedrooms and five baths**.
