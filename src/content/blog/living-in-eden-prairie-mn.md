**What's it like to live in Eden Prairie? I filmed this from Red Rock Lake Park (squirrels and all) to share the lakes, parks, food and commute that make this southwest metro city so popular.**

Figures are from when I made the video above (October 2022). See our [Eden Prairie community guide](/communities/eden-prairie) for current numbers, and read [Eden Prairie will never be the same](/blog/eden-prairie-light-rail-redevelopment) for what's changing now.

## Quick facts

Eden Prairie is on the southwest side of the Minneapolis metro, with about **64,000 people** and an average commute of about **24 minutes**. The median home price was about **$450,000**, a strong value compared with similar cities around the country, given everything you get.

## Lakes

Eden Prairie has **15 lakes** for recreation in every season. **Bryant Lake** and **Mitchell Lake** are full recreational lakes for boating, water skiing, wakeboarding and wake surfing, and the smaller lakes add a lot of value to nearby homes. A lake makes a great neighbor.

## Parks

There are **37 parks**, including **Bryant Lake Regional Park** and **Purgatory Park**. At **Red Rock Lake Park**, where I filmed, people take out pontoons and fish in summer, and in winter you'll see hockey rinks on the ice, because this is the State of Hockey. Across the parks you'll find cross-country skiing, tennis courts and reservable picnic areas for a cookout.

## Food

Start at **Eden Prairie Center**, an enclosed mall with plenty of restaurants. (Fun fact: the first enclosed mall in the country was Southdale, in neighboring Edina; we need them because it gets really cold.) Beyond the mall, there's everything from **Redstone** to sushi at **Kona Grill** to the **5-8 Tavern**, from steaks to greasy cheeseburgers, often within walking distance of home.

## Getting around

Eden Prairie has great freeway access: **Highway 212** on the south side, and **I-494** and **Highway 169** on the north. Buses connect transit stations to downtown and around the metro, and the **Green Line Extension** light rail, under construction when I made this video, ends in Eden Prairie and is now set to open in 2027.
