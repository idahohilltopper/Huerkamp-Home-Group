**If you drove in the Twin Cities this year, you felt it. In the video above I walk through every major road project around the metro: what's being built, when it ends, and whether it should change where you buy.**

Dates and figures are as stated in the video, as of November 2026 when I made it. Construction schedules change, so check MnDOT for the latest on your route.

## How bad was it?

According to TomTom, which tracks traffic in cities around the world, Twin Cities drivers lost about **39 hours** to rush-hour traffic last year, almost four more than the year before. A six-mile drive took almost **13 minutes**, 43 seconds slower. We still ranked right in the middle of US cities; we just notice it because we're used to traffic moving. My take: a lot of big projects landed at the same time, and more people are back in the office.

## South of the river: all three crossings had work

For most south-metro drivers, the ways north across the Minnesota River are **Highway 169, I-35W and Cedar Avenue (Highway 77)**, and all three had work this year.

- **Cedar Avenue:** repaving from Apple Valley to the river bridge in Eagan, plus fixes to 10 bridges near I-494. Work started in July and runs through **November 2028**. Next year, both directions will share one side of the road.
- **I-35W in Burnsville:** from Cliff Road south to where 35W and 35E split, including a new Cliff Road bridge. Cliff Road under 35W has been closed since the spring of last year, and the contractor on one bridge was fired. The state said 35W should be done this fall; it wasn't finished yet when I made the video. This is the one I expect to ease first.
- **Highway 169:** a lighter year, with lane closures on the river bridge in Shakopee that finished in early September.

If you commute north from Lakeville, Burnsville, Apple Valley, Shakopee, Eagan or Prior Lake, you felt every bit of it. See [Burnsville](/communities/burnsville), [Apple Valley](/communities/apple-valley) and [Eagan](/communities/eagan).

## I-494: E-ZPass lanes by 2030

I-494 is the main east-west highway across the south metro, right past the airport. **Phase one** (started spring 2023) adds E-ZPass lanes between Highway 100 and I-35W plus a new ramp between 35W and 494, wrapping up this fall. **Phase two** starts next spring and runs to **fall 2030**. By then there will be E-ZPass lanes from Highway 169 to Highway 77: pay to use them when traffic is heavy, or ride free with a passenger. The state is also rebuilding interchanges at 82nd Street, 35W and Highway 77. The whole rebuild costs about **$750 million**, with a **$135 million** federal grant covering part of it. Together, that's about eight construction seasons in a row.

## I-94 between Minneapolis and St. Paul: the biggest, and furthest away

This 7.5-mile stretch has **four options** on the table: leave it alone, keep it in good repair, or two plans that shrink or reshape the freeway. A street-level boulevard was dropped last year. One early estimate puts the rebuild at **$1.5 billion to $2.5 billion**. The state hopes to pick a plan in 2028, and construction likely won't start until the end of the decade, so it won't change your commute any time soon.

## North and west metro

- **Highway 65 in Blaine:** four stoplights become interchanges at 99th, 105th, 109th and 117th, for about **$195 million**. Work started in June and runs through **summer 2030**, with frontage roads and a new walking bridge. A stop-and-go road becomes free-flowing. See [Blaine](/communities/blaine).
- **Highway 252 in Brooklyn Park and Brooklyn Center:** the state wants a freeway with three full interchanges. Still in planning; construction could start around **2029**. See [Brooklyn Park](/communities/brooklyn-park).
- **Highway 280** between St. Paul and Roseville: closed this spring and summer, reopened in late August; more bridge work is planned for 2028.
- **Highway 12** between Wayzata and Minnetonka: a **$37.5 million** project through November, with part of the road fully rebuilt and up to 20 ramps repaired.
- **Highway 97 in Forest Lake**, from I-35 to Highway 61: turn lanes, roundabouts and a new trail, set to finish this fall. See [Forest Lake](/communities/forest-lake).

## Should you avoid buying near these roads?

A lot of people ask us this. My answer is **no**. I'm not seeing prices near these corridors take a hit, and they may improve once traffic flows better. This work had to happen, and it's temporary.

**If you're buying:**
1. **Drive the commute at rush hour** before you commit.
2. **Find out when the road work ends.** A spot that's painful to reach today could be much easier by the time you settle in.

**If you're selling:** buyers will ask. Have the end date ready (your agent should know it), and tell buyers what your commute is normally like without the construction.

## The recap

Right now traffic is rough. I-35W in Burnsville should ease first, and the rest runs in stages through 2030. The commute you test today won't be your commute in two years, so keep that in mind when you pick your home.
