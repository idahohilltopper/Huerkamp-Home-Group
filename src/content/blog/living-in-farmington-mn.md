**Is Farmington a popular place to live, even though it's on the edge of the Twin Cities? Here's what you need to know about this south metro city, from the parks to the commute to home prices. (I filmed the video above in our first October snow; that's Minnesota.)**

Figures are from when I made the video in October 2022. See our [Farmington community guide](/communities/farmington) for current numbers.

## Where Farmington is

Farmington sits directly south of Saint Paul in Dakota County, about **35 minutes from Saint Paul** and **40 to 45 minutes from Minneapolis**, with a median commute of about 30 minutes. **Highway 3** runs through the middle of town north to Saint Paul and south toward Northfield, and **I-35** is just to the west. Neighbors include Lakeville to the west, Apple Valley and Rosemount to the north, and Castle Rock and Hampton to the east.

Farmington had a little over **23,000 people** in just under 8,000 households, and almost **85%** of homes were owner-occupied, a very high rate.

Most people get around by car, but the **Minnesota Valley Transit Authority** serves the south metro and connects to light rail for a Twins or Vikings game or a night out.

## Parks and trails

Farmington has about **913 acres** of parks and open space, **48 miles of trails** and **27 parks**.

- **Whitetail Woods Regional Park**, owned by Dakota County, is almost 450 acres with an amphitheater, picnic shelters, a sledding hill, trails, a dog park and secluded cabins you can rent by the night.
- **Rambling River Park** (about 76 acres) is a main city park, with playgrounds and softball fields.

There aren't big lakes in town, but the Vermillion River runs through Farmington on its way to the Mississippi, and Lakeville's lakes are just west. And in Minnesota, any frozen pond eventually becomes a hockey rink.

## Food

Beyond the usual chains, a favorite of mine is **Bourbon Butcher Kitchen & Bar**, with craft cocktails and great smoked food. **Farmington Tap House** and **Farmington Steakhouse** sit side by side for a fun night out.

## Home prices

The median home value was about **$350,000** when I made the video, one of the more affordable options we'd covered in the Twin Cities. See [Expo 2031](/blog/expo-2031-dakota-county-worlds-fair) for one reason more people are watching Farmington now.
