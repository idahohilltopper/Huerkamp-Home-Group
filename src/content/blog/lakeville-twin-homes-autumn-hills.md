**Twin homes are an underrated alternative to a detached house. In the video above, I toured Country Joe Homes' rambler-style twin home at Autumn Hills in Lakeville: two bedrooms and two baths on about 2,000 square feet, all on one level, plus a huge basement.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (spring 2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The main floor

- A **wide entry** (at least 6 feet) with tall ceilings.
- A **flex room** off the entry that counts as a bedroom, across from a full bath with a shower.
- An **owner's entry** from the garage with lockers, and the laundry connected to the owner's closet.
- A **kitchen** with an enormous island seating four or five, 42-inch upper cabinets, matching counters, an undermount sink, Frigidaire Gallery stainless appliances and a pantry.
- A **living room** with a tiled fireplace surround and lots of natural light, flowing into the dining area.
- A **coat closet** for guests.

## The owner's suite

A mini-vaulted ceiling that's close to 10 feet, room for a California king, and a bath with dual vanities, a shower, a soaking tub, attractive new sheet vinyl and a large water closet. The walk-in closet is huge.

## The basement

The unfinished basement is enormous, with room for a couple of bedrooms, a family room, a movie room and storage, and it's already plumbed for another bathroom. Finished, the home could approach **4,000 square feet**. Finishing it was estimated at about **$75,000 to $100,000** for roughly 1,800 more finished square feet.

## Why twin homes?

You get **one-level living** with a garage and yard at a lower price than many detached homes. Country Joe Homes is a family-owned builder that has built a lot in the south and east metro, especially Lakeville, for about 40 years. This model was priced in the **mid-$500,000s** and was one of their hottest sellers.
