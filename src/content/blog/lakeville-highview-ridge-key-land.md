**In the video above, I toured a Key Land Homes two-story at Highview Ridge in Lakeville: five bedrooms, four baths, about 2,900 finished square feet and a three-car garage, including a finished basement, for about $635,000.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (winter 2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## Why Lakeville

[Lakeville](/communities/lakeville), my hometown, has about 70,000 people now (it was about 13,000 when I was born). It's roughly 15 minutes to the airport and 20 to 25 minutes to either downtown in normal traffic, with easy access to I-35 and Cedar Avenue. It's known for **Lake Marion, Orchard Lake and Crystal Lake** and an unbelievable number of parks and trails. See my [Lakeville driving tour](/blog/lakeville-driving-tour).

## The main floor

- An entry with a **3/4 bath**, handy for guests.
- A **living room** with 9-foot ceilings, lots of recessed lighting and a linear fireplace, big enough for about eight people.
- A **kitchen** with gold-tone chandeliers, a gas range, stainless double-door fridge and a single-basin undermount sink.
- An **office with a closet** that could easily become a main-floor bedroom.
- A **drop zone** with a bench off the garage.

It's bright even on an overcast winter day.

## Upstairs

- An **owner's suite** with two closets, including a walk-in, and wider windows that bring in a lot of light. The bath has double sinks, a soaking tub and a shower.
- **Two more bedrooms** (either could be an office) and a full bath.
- No loft, which helps hit the price point.

The yard is big, but this particular lot backs up to a busier road. The builder planted pines that should help buffer noise as they grow. Always check what's behind a lot.

## The lower level

This is a **lookout** basement: big windows you can see out of, but no door. (A walkout has a door to the yard; an egress basement has windows in window wells.) It had a family room, **two more bedrooms**, a bath and good storage.

## The value

Five bedrooms, four baths, about 2,900 square feet, a three-car garage and a finished basement for about **$635,000** was a good deal for Lakeville.
