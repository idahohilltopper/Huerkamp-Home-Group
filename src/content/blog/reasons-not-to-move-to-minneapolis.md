**Here are 10 reasons you might *not* want to move to Minneapolis, and why every one of them has an upside.**

## 1. The cold

Yes, winters can be very cold. The upside: the cold keeps away venomous snakes, giant spiders and plenty of other things you don't want. And we get four full seasons. You do have to enjoy snow to some degree, whether that's ice fishing, snowmobiling, skiing or a good snowball fight. In exchange, summers from about mid-May to mid-September turn the place into the Emerald City, and fall color here is spectacular.

## 2. Bugs

No giant cockroaches. Our biggest bug issue is mosquitoes, thanks to all those lakes, and Metropolitan Mosquito Control works hard to keep them in check.

## 3. If you hate wildlife

Deer everywhere, and fishing everywhere. If you don't like wildlife, this isn't the place for you.

## 4. Allergies

Trees, flowers and pollen can make allergy season rough. Stock up on your allergy medicine of choice.

## 5. If you love traffic

Compared with most big metros, Minneapolis has very little traffic, plus buses and a growing light rail system. If you love sitting in traffic, look elsewhere.

## 6. It's spread out

From one side of the metro to the other can be about an hour and a half. The upside: lots of suburbs and lots of room to spread your wings.

## 7. Road construction

Freeze-thaw cycles wreck roads, so we fix them constantly. The joke is that Minnesota has two seasons: winter and road construction. The upside: well-maintained roads, and even after a foot of snow, you're usually back to highway speed the next day.

## 8. If you don't like friendly people

Minnesota nice is real. Expect conversations at the grocery store, neighbors who wave and block parties.

## 9. Hail and the occasional tornado

Hail is part of our weather; the joke is your roof will get hit about every 10 years. Tornadoes are more common farther south, closer to Iowa, but they happen.

## 10. Taxes

State income tax rates run from about 5% to nearly 10%. What you get in return is one of the cleanest, best-run places I've ever been, with clean water, maintained roads and quick snow removal.
