**In the video above, I toured a Key Land Homes model at Sapphire Lake in Farmington: four bedrooms, three baths, about 2,100 finished square feet and a four-car garage. Its great room is something I rarely see at this price.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (filmed in winter, published June 2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## Where it is

[Farmington](/communities/farmington) is one of the farthest southeast suburbs in the metro, but it's an easy commute: Highway 52 is close, I-35 is about 10 minutes west, the airport is under 30 minutes away and both downtowns are under 40. More in [living in Farmington](/blog/living-in-farmington-mn).

## The main floor

- **A great room with ceilings around 14 feet**, huge windows and a statement chandelier. Rooms like this usually show up in million-dollar homes.
- A linear **gas fireplace** with a built-in bench as the focal point.
- **Kitchen:** a five-burner gas cooktop, solid-surface counters in a two-tone look, soft-close cabinets, a big island that seats about five, and one of the widest windows over a sink I've seen.
- A **south-facing back** that floods the home with light, even on a snowy January day.

## Upstairs

- **Four bedrooms, two baths and the laundry room** all on one level.
- An **owner's suite** overlooking a protected wetland, with a good-size bath and a closet with windows.
- A **shared bath with dual vanities** and a separate room for the tub and toilet, so two people can get ready at once.
- A walking trail runs behind the home, and the backyard is nearly 100 feet deep.

## The lower level

The **lookout basement** was unfinished, roughed in for a bathroom, with room for another bedroom, a family room and a wet bar. Finishing a basement like this typically runs about **$45,000 to $60,000**.

## What it cost

This model was available for about **$590,000**. For a great room like this, that's a lot of home for the money.
