**Oak Creek is one of D.R. Horton's newer developments in Chaska, with about 70 lots remaining and several inventory homes when I filmed. In the video above, I toured the Redwood model: about 3,100 square feet above ground plus an unfinished walkout basement.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (November 2024). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The location

Many lots at Oak Creek are larger, have great views or both. This model backs up to a **wetland** with mature trees, so it feels like an established neighborhood. Shopping and a hospital are about five minutes away, **Highway 212** makes commuting easy, and the airport is roughly 20 minutes away. See [Chaska](/communities/chaska).

## The main floor

- A **front office** that's a good size without being too big, and a sitting or dining room.
- **Dual kitchen islands**, the Redwood's signature feature, with the gourmet package: a statement hood, a KitchenAid five-burner gas cooktop, ovens and dishwasher, a Whirlpool fridge, gold hardware and blue-gray cabinets.
- A **second office or homework nook** off the kitchen.
- A **two-sided fireplace** and wide hallways and stairs.
- A coat area and half bath off the garage.

## Upstairs

- A **massive loft** and an upstairs **laundry room** with blue-gray cabinets.
- **Three bedrooms** with walk-in closets: two share a bath with a private toilet-and-tub room, and one has its own bath next to it.
- An **enormous owner's suite** with room for a sitting area, a soaking tub, a large walk-in shower, a private water closet and a closet with a window and automatic lights.

## The lower level

The **walkout** basement was unfinished but planned for a family room, a bedroom and a bath, adding about 1,000 to 1,200 square feet for roughly **$60,000** at the same finish level. D.R. Horton's single-family homes here also include **smart-home** controls and a **tankless water heater**.

## What it cost

The Redwood at Oak Creek was priced around **$690,000**.
