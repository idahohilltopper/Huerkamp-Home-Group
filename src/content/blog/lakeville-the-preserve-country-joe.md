**The Preserve is an exclusive Country Joe Homes community in Lakeville, with homes ranging from about $800,000 to $1.5 million. In the video above, I toured one of their premier models: about 4,800 square feet with six bedrooms and five baths.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (January 2025). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The community

Country Joe is the exclusive builder at The Preserve. When I filmed, the first phase had sold out and lots remained in the second and third. It's about 20 minutes to the airport or downtown Minneapolis, about 15 minutes to the Mall of America, and close to Cedar Avenue and I-35W. There are walking and bike trails in the neighborhood, fences are allowed, and siding is maintenance-free. Lakeville's **Airlake** industrial park also helps keep property taxes in check. See my [Lakeville driving tour](/blog/lakeville-driving-tour).

## The main floor

- A **grand entry** in whites and grays with warm wood accents.
- A **kitchen** with a big island, a statement range hood, a five-burner cooktop, custom soft-close cabinets and a Frigidaire Gallery appliance package.
- A **traditional gas fireplace** (I prefer it to the skinny linear ones) and 9-foot ceilings with lots of recessed lighting.
- An **8-foot sliding door** to a colored-concrete patio with room for a fire pit.
- A **butler's pantry** with its own sink and dishwasher, ideal for catered parties.
- A **mudroom** with a bench off the three-car garage.
- A **main-floor flex room** that works as a bedroom, office or den, next to a 3/4 bath with a tiled shower.

Woodwork is enameled on site, the interior doors are solid core, and the windows are Andersen.

## Upstairs

- A **loft**.
- A **bedroom with its own en-suite bath**.
- Two bedrooms sharing a **Jack-and-Jill bath** with two vanities and a separate tiled tub/shower room.
- **Laundry** on the same level.
- An **owner's suite** with a sitting area, a fully tiled bath with dual vanities, a soaking tub, a shower with a bench, and a custom closet (not just wire shelving).

## The lower level

A wide-open family room with good natural light, a large egress bedroom, another bath, plenty of storage and **zoned heating** to keep each level comfortable.

## What it cost

This six-bedroom, five-bath model was priced around **$1,115,000**, and Country Joe had been selling quite a few homes at or above $1 million there.
