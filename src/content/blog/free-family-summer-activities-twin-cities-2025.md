**Summer is officially here**—and whether you’re already settled into your new home or just getting to know the area, there are **plenty of ways for families to celebrate the end of the school year without breaking the bank**. From free treats to splash pad adventures, the **Minneapolis–St. Paul metro** is packed with fun, free ways to welcome the season.

## 🍪 End-of-School Treats for Kids & Grads

**Buca di Beppo** – Graduates get a **free cannoli** when dining in.  
**Insomnia Cookies** – Through June 30, grads in cap and gown get a **free cookie** with purchase.

## 🍦 Ice Cream Socials & Free Cone Days

**Dairy Queen** – Participating locations offer **Free Cone Days**. Check local DQ Facebook pages for dates.

**Free Ice Cream Events** – Hosted by Minneapolis Parks:

**McRae Park**: June 5, 6–8 PM

**Bottineau Field Park**: June 10, 6–8 PM + movie

**MLK Rec Center**: June 11, 6–8 PM w/ Sebastian Joe’s

## 🎳 Kids Bowl Free Program

Kids registered through [KidsBowlFree.com](https://www.kidsbowlfree.com/) get **2 free games of bowling per day** all summer at participating centers.

## 🎨 Arts & Culture Highlights

**MIA** (Minneapolis): Always free. Don’t miss Family Days (2nd Sundays).

**Walker Art Center**: Free First Saturdays + Sculpture Garden

**Como Zoo** (St. Paul): Free admission, splash pad, gardens & zoo

## 🌳 Outdoor Nature Play

**Minnehaha Falls**: Waterfall views, trails, splash pad (Minneapolis)

**Tamarack Nature Center**: Muddy fun in Discovery Hollow (White Bear Township)

**Hyland Park Reserve**: Huge playground + trails (Bloomington)

## 📚 Library Summer Programs

Hennepin, Ramsey, Scott & Dakota County libraries offer:

Storytimes, take-home kits, and **summer reading rewards** (like Twins tickets!)

## 🎶 Free Events & Festivals

**Movies in the Parks**: Titles like *Moana*, *Elemental*, *Super Mario Bros.*  
Full schedule → [Minneapolis Parks](https://www.minneapolisparks.org/activities__events/events/movies_in_the_parks/)

**Open Streets Minneapolis**: First event on **June 9 (Lyndale Ave)**  
[Event calendar](https://www.openstreetsmpls.org/)

**WaterFest – Lake Phalen**: **May 31**, **2025** – free activities, canoeing, crafts  
[More info](https://www.visitsaintpaul.com/blog/hang-out-at-these-summer-events-in-saint-paul)

## 🏖️ Splash Pads & Beaches

**Eagan Central Park Splash Pad** – Shaded & picnic-ready

**North Commons Water Park (Mpls)** – Waterslides & splash zone

**Lake Nokomis Beach** – Swim & sand, right in the city

**Tip**: Many of these promotions are time-sensitive and may require proof of graduation or age eligibility. Always check with the specific location or organization for the most current information before attending.
