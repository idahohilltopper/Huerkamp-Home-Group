**Twin Cities homes start around $300,000, and one is listed at $50 million. Most people only see one end of that range. Here's every price tier, so you can see what fits your budget.**

Prices are as of my video above (May 2026).

## Tier 1: about $200,000 to $350,000

- **[Richfield](/communities/richfield)**, directly south of Minneapolis: close in without city pricing, with homes typically in the mid-$300,000s, walkable neighborhoods and bike lanes. **Wood Lake Nature Center** (about 150 acres with boardwalk trails, wildlife and winter snowshoeing) is one of my favorite places to walk, and Veterans Park adds a lake, an ice rink and mini golf. Prices have stayed relatively flat while other suburbs climbed, which is the opportunity. Trade-off: smaller lots and a city-suburb feel. I've owned rentals there since 2008 and 2009, and I wouldn't hold them that long if I didn't believe in it.
- **[Northfield](/communities/northfield)**, about 40 miles south on the Cannon River: small-town energy, coffee shops, breweries, local restaurants, and St. Olaf and Carleton colleges. Homes run in the mid to high $300,000s, with a bit less competition. Trade-off: the commute.
- **[Bloomington](/communities/bloomington)**, about 10 miles south of downtown, with about 88,000 people and highways in every direction: the Mall of America (500-plus stores, Nickelodeon Universe, Sea Life and a water park on the way) and the Minnesota Valley National Wildlife Refuge along the river. The middle of the market is in the mid to high $300,000s, and it moves fast.

## Tier 2: about $350,000 to $500,000, the busiest range

- **[Maple Grove](/communities/maple-grove)**: one of the most in-demand suburbs, with homes from the mid-$400,000s. The community center is expanding (a new ice sheet, pools and fitness space), a new Life Time is coming, and **Elm Creek Park Reserve** is one of the metro's largest parks. Trade-off: very car-dependent.
- **[Savage](/communities/savage)**, about 15 miles southwest, with about 33,000 people and a strong community feel: Savage Community Park (pickleball, fishing and fields) and **Murphy-Hanrehan Park Reserve** (mountain biking, skiing, hiking and horse trails). Homes run in the low $400,000s. Trade-off: more driving.

## Tier 3: about $500,000 to $750,000

- **[Woodbury](/communities/woodbury)**, about 20 miles east, with about 83,000 people: homes from the low $400,000s, with single-family homes quickly reaching the mid-$500,000s. **Central Park** just finished a roughly $41 million renovation with an amphitheater and an indoor playground. About 3,500 acres of parkland and eight lakes with public access. Trade-off: a longer drive west.
- **[Lakeville](/communities/lakeville)**, my hometown, about 25 miles south, with about 81,000 people: high $400,000s to low $500,000s and up, more than 100 parks, about 140 miles of trails and Lake Marion. Homes are staying on the market a little longer, so there's a bit less competition, but great homes still move fast.
- **[Chanhassen](/communities/chanhassen)**, named Money's #1 best place to live in 2021, about 30 minutes from downtown: 12 lakes, 66-plus miles of trails and Prince's **Paisley Park**, with a new community center in the works. Homes start in the high $500,000s and easily reach $1 million.

## Tier 4: about $750,000 and up

- **[Edina](/communities/edina)**, about 10 miles from Minneapolis, with 50th & France (175 shops and restaurants) at its heart. Homes average in the high $700,000s, and many areas run well past $1 million. The **METRO E Line** makes getting downtown without a car easier.
- **[Medina](/communities/medina)**, about 18 miles west: rural, with large lots, open land and acreage estates. Values start in the low $800,000s and reach many millions, close to Maple Grove and Plymouth shopping but car-dependent.

## Tier 5: about $1 million to $5 million

- **Bde Maka Ska** (renamed in 2018 from Lake Calhoun), the largest lake in [Minneapolis](/communities/minneapolis): active and social, with Uptown just east. Listings range from condos to $1 million-plus homes across the parkway and trail from the water (no direct lake access).
- **[Orono](/communities/orono)**, on Lake Minnetonka (about 8,200 people): privacy and land, with averages in the low millions, low inventory and strong demand.
- **Lake of the Isles** in Minneapolis: historic homes, lake views and connected trails, with listings from about $400,000 condos to about $8 million estates.

## Tier 6: $5 million and up

**Lake Minnetonka** leads the state in pricing. [Wayzata](/communities/wayzata), on the northeast shore about 11 miles from Minneapolis, has a walkable downtown that feels like the Hamptons or Nantucket, with lakefront dining and boating on 14,000-plus acres of water. The **Lake Point estate** (8 acres, 650 feet of shoreline) is listed at **$50 million**, the most expensive listing in Minnesota history.
