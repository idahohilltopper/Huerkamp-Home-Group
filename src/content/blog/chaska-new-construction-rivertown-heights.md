**In the video above, I toured the Jordan model at Rivertown Heights, a D.R. Horton community in Chaska. At the time, it had 64 lots left. Here's what stood out, and what it cost.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (late 2024). Builder plans, lots and prices change, so check current availability.

## The main floor

- **Wide-open entry and dining area**, which could also work as an office.
- **Kitchen:** white cabinets with brushed-silver hardware, a stainless appliance package with double ovens, stone countertops, an undermount sink and a big walk-in pantry.
- **Living room** that flows into the kitchen and dining area, with a walkout lower level and good views out back.
- **Smart-home features** standard: thermostat and controls you can adjust from your phone.
- **A main-floor bedroom with a full bath**, great for guests or anyone who'd rather skip the stairs. Not every builder offers that.

## Upstairs

- A **huge loft** with room for a crowd.
- **Four bedrooms**, a shared bath and an upstairs laundry room.
- An **owner's suite** sized just right, with dual vanities, a soaking tub, a separate shower, a private water closet and two closets.
- Lots of **natural light** throughout, which matters on cloudy Minnesota winter days.

## What it cost

The Jordan model was priced around **$650,000** for about **3,000 square feet**, five bedrooms and three bathrooms. Finishing the basement added roughly **$50,000 to $60,000** and about 1,200 more square feet. There were also inventory homes ready sooner.
