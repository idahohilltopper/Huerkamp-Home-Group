Highway 13 between Savage and Burnsville is about to become a multi-year construction zone. MnDOT plans to [reconstruct Hwy 13 and its intersections "between Quentin Ave. in Savage and Nicollet Ave. in Burnsville"](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/metro/projects/hwy13savageburnsville/index.html), starting with a new interchange at Nicollet Avenue in spring 2027. If you live, work or are thinking about buying near that stretch, here's what MnDOT has published about what changes, and when.

## The schedule

MnDOT's project page lists two phases:

- **Nicollet Ave. interchange (Burnsville):** spring 2027 through fall 2028.
- **Quentin Ave. to Washburn Ave. (Savage into Burnsville):** spring 2028 through fall 2030.

MnDOT's project page says that during the Nicollet work, Nicollet Avenue will be closed at Hwy 13 with posted detours, and Hwy 13 will be reduced to one lane in each direction. The cost is listed as "TBD." (MnDOT's background page still describes the next phase as "tentatively scheduled for 2027-2028"; the dated schedule above is from the project's main page.)

## What gets built at each intersection

From MnDOT's [design layouts](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/metro/projects/hwy13savageburnsville/design.html), presented at a public meeting on Jan. 27, 2026:

**Nicollet Ave. (Burnsville).** A full interchange: a Hwy 13 bridge over Nicollet, with signals at the on- and off-ramps.

**Quentin Ave. (Savage).** A Hwy 13 bridge over Quentin, exit and entrance ramps, and a roundabout at Quentin Ave. and 123rd St. West, the western way into downtown Savage.

**Huntington Ave.** Listed by MnDOT as a new road. A Hwy 13 bridge over Huntington, a roundabout at Huntington and the frontage road, a ramp from westbound Hwy 13 to Huntington Ave. and downtown Savage, and a ramp from Huntington onto westbound Hwy 13.

**Chowen Ave.** A Hwy 13 bridge over Chowen, a roundabout at Chowen and the frontage road, and a ramp from eastbound Hwy 13 to Chowen and downtown Savage.

**Washburn Ave. (Burnsville).** A roundabout at Washburn and the frontage road, a ramp from westbound Hwy 13 to Washburn for business access, and a ramp from Washburn onto eastbound Hwy 13.

Along the way, MnDOT plans to build or rebuild frontage roads between Chowen and Washburn and to add trails and sidewalks. The project's stated benefits include better traffic flow and "improve pedestrian and bike access."

In other words, the traffic signals along this stretch give way to bridges, ramps and roundabouts, with Hwy 13 itself passing over the cross streets.

## Why MnDOT is doing it

MnDOT [calls the corridor](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/metro/projects/hwy13savageburnsville/background.html) "the primary east-west connection between Hwy 169 and I-35W south on the Minnesota River," and says efficient freight access to the Ports of Savage is "critical for statewide economic success." Public input, MnDOT says, pointed to the need for better traffic flow, less congestion, better pedestrian facilities and better local access. An earlier phase rebuilt the Dakota Ave. interchange with a new bridge, new ramps and realigned frontage roads; MnDOT puts that project's cost at $32 million.

The city has its own reasons to care: Savage's [Downtown Redevelopment Vision](https://letstalksavage.com/downtown-redevelopment-vision), approved by the City Council on Dec. 16, 2024, says "Highway 13 is both our greatest asset and challenge," and calls for a more welcoming western entrance at Quentin Avenue and 123rd Street tied to the Hwy 13 changes.

## Noise walls: where they're going, and where they're not

Neighbors along the corridor voted this spring on proposed noise walls. Voting ran from Feb. 25 to April 27, 2026. Only owners and residents who would get at least a five-decibel reduction could vote, weighted by how much the wall affects their property. MnDOT's [results](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/metro/projects/hwy13savageburnsville/noisewalls.html), as of April 28, 2026:

| Wall | Location | Yes | No | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | South side of Hwy 13, Quentin Ave. to Lynn Ave., Savage | 6% | 61% | Not built |
| 7 | South side, between I-35W and Nicollet Ave., Burnsville | 41% | 14% | Will be built |
| 8 | South side, just east of Nicollet Ave., Burnsville | 4% | 67% | Not built |
| 9 | South side, Nicollet Ave. to Portland Ave., Burnsville | 82% | 5% | Will be built |
| 10 | North side, Nicollet Ave. to Portland Ave., Burnsville | 54% | 19% | Will be built, about 500 feet shorter on the west end |

Percentages are shares of eligible voting points; the rest didn't vote. MnDOT says a wall that was voted down is not reconsidered.

## What it means if you're buying or selling nearby

- **Buying near the corridor?** Look at where the home sits against the design layouts, the construction years for that stretch, and whether a noise wall is planned there. A house near the Quentin to Lynn stretch, for example, won't get the wall that was voted down.
- **Selling in the next few years?** Construction seasons near the Nicollet interchange (2027 to 2028) and the Savage stretch (2028 to 2030) can mean detours and noise for showings. Buyers will ask, so it helps to know the schedule.
- **Commuting?** Plan on detours around Nicollet Ave. during the interchange work and one lane each way on Hwy 13 through the work zone.

MnDOT updates the schedule as the project moves along, so check the [project page](https://www.dot.state.mn.us/metro/projects/hwy13savageburnsville/index.html) before you count on a date.
