**Caramore Crossing is a D.R. Horton community in Rosemount with shopping close by. In the video above, I toured the Whitney: about 3,400 square feet with five bedrooms, four baths, a three-car garage and an unfinished walkout basement.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (December 2024). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The main floor

- A **dining room** off the entry and wide, open hallways.
- A **gourmet kitchen package** with KitchenAid appliances, a five-burner cooktop, a huge island topped with a single piece of solid surface, a Kohler undermount sink, a walk-in pantry and a coffee bar.
- A **living room** with a fireplace and views to a small pond out back.
- A **main-floor bedroom with a 3/4 bath**, a feature D.R. Horton is including in more of its single-family plans.
- A coat closet off the garage.
- **Smart-home features** in every home in the community, controlled from your phone.

## Upstairs

- A **big loft**.
- An **owner's suite** that's spacious without wasted space, with a shower, soaking tub, wide dual vanity, a private water closet and two closets.
- **Three more bedrooms**, each with a walk-in closet, including one surprisingly large bedroom, plus a Jack-and-Jill-style bath with separate vanities and a door to the tub area.
- An upstairs **laundry room** with cabinets and folding space.

## The lower level

The **walkout** basement was unfinished. Finishing it was estimated at about **$40,000**, adding around 1,200 square feet with room for a family room, a bedroom, a bath and storage.

## What it cost

The Whitney at Caramore Crossing was priced around **$690,000**.
