**We get lots of questions about Burnsville, so I headed to Crystal Beach Park, with Crystal Lake in front of me and Buck Hill on the horizon, to share what living there is like.**

Figures are from when I made the video above (October 2022). See our [Burnsville community guide](/communities/burnsville) for current numbers.

## Where Burnsville is

Burnsville sits directly south of Minneapolis, just south of the Minnesota River, about **20 to 30 minutes** from downtown and the sports venues. The average commute was about **24 minutes**. Neighbors include Bloomington to the north, Eagan and Apple Valley to the east, and Savage to the west.

Burnsville had about **64,000 people** in about 24,000 households, and about **68%** of homes were owner-occupied. The median home value was in the **high $300,000s**, still an affordable option in the metro.

## Getting around

**I-35W, I-35E and Cedar Avenue** are all close by. The Minnesota Valley Transit Authority runs buses along I-35, and the **METRO Orange Line** bus rapid transit runs from Burnsville straight into downtown Minneapolis. Even after a foot of snow, you're usually back up to speed on the freeway the next day.

## Parks, lakes and a ski hill

Burnsville has **76 parks**, with hiking, biking and cross-country ski trails, skating rinks, playgrounds, disc golf and athletic fields, plus around **300 bodies of water**, from ponds to the Minnesota River.

- **Crystal Lake**, the biggest at about 700 acres, has a public boat launch, a swimming beach, a park and fishing piers, so you can fish without a boat.
- **Sunset Pond** has a walking trail just over a mile around, with lots of nature.
- **Buck Hill** has the biggest vertical drop of any ski hill in the Twin Cities. Lindsey Vonn got her start there, along with other Olympic downhill racers.

## Food

There's something for every palate: **Jensen's Cafe** for breakfast and brunch, **Porter Creek Hardwood Grill** for craft cocktails and great smoked wings, **Chianti Grill**, the Greek **Mediterranean Cruise Cafe**, **Cameron's Bay** and the **Buffalo Tap**.

## Is Burnsville for you?

A central south metro location, easy freeway and transit access, lakes and a ski hill in town, at prices that are still within reach for many buyers.
