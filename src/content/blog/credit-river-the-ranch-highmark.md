**The Ranch in Credit River offers acreage lots of about 2.5 acres minimum. In the video above, I toured a HighMark Builders custom rambler: about 4,300 square feet (2,600 on the main level) with four bedrooms, five baths and a heated, insulated three-car garage.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (November 2024). Lot availability changes, so check what's current. For another builder here, see [the Cuddigan home at The Ranch](/blog/credit-river-the-ranch-cuddigan).

## One-level living, no expense spared

This is a **rambler**: all main living on one level. From the custom front-door handle to the light fixtures, every detail was thought out.

- A **living room** with floor-to-ceiling windows about 10 feet tall, black frames against white walls, a custom gas fireplace, oak cabinets and floors, built-in speakers and a **smooth ceiling** (one of the more expensive finishes because it shows every imperfection).
- A **kitchen** with a **seamless granite island**, a six-burner **Thermador** range with a griddle, a custom hood, a wine cabinet, a **hidden panel-ready fridge, freezer and dishwasher**, a pull-out trash, and air switches for the disposal.
- An **eat-in dining area** that seats six to eight and a sitting room with built-in bookshelves, catching both sunrise and sunset.
- A **butler's pantry** with the microwave, a beverage fridge and lots of cabinetry.
- A **garage** with a heater, floor drains and plenty of light, plus a mudroom with a bench and a large coat closet.
- A **laundry room** with solid-surface counters, a hanging bar, a pull-out faucet and push-to-open cabinets.
- **8-foot solid-core doors** throughout.

## Bedrooms

- A **home office with 7-foot windows** that could also be a bedroom, with its own tiled walk-in shower.
- An **owner's suite** facing south over a stone retaining wall, with a big walk-in closet with windows and built-ins, a soaking tub with a hand sprayer, a walk-in shower with a bench, and a private water closet.

## The lower level

A **lookout** basement with 9-foot ceilings, luxury vinyl plank floors, a custom plate rail, a big family room, a hidden **exercise room**, another full bath, under-stair storage and **two more bedrooms** sharing a bath with separate compartments. Heating and cooling are **dual-zone**.

Outside, the lot is just over 2 acres with full irrigation and boulder retaining walls, and the back looks as good as the front.
