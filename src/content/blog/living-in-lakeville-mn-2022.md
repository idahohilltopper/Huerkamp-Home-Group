**If you enjoy city life but don't want to live in Minneapolis or Saint Paul, Lakeville may be the perfect spot. It's the city I grew up in, and here's my look at what it's like to live there.**

Figures are as of my video above (fall 2022). For a newer look, see my [2026 Lakeville driving tour](/blog/lakeville-driving-tour) and [why Lakeville is the most underrated suburb](/blog/lakeville-mn-most-underrated-suburb).

## The basics

- **Population:** just under **73,000** at the time, growing fast.
- **Commute:** about **30 to 35 minutes** to either downtown via I-35W or I-35E.
- **Neighbors:** Apple Valley, Burnsville, Elko New Market and Credit River.
- **Home prices:** a median sale price around **$470,000**, with homes from about $200,000 up to $2 million.

## The lakes

**Lake Marion**, just over 500 acres, is a full-recreation lake with swimming beaches. **Orchard Lake**, a bit north, is a surprisingly good muskie lake. Both have public beaches and boat launches, and both are great for winter panfishing through the ice. Want your own dock and evening cruises without waiting at the public launch? ## Parks and trails

Lakeville has about **125 miles** of pedestrian and bike trails, roughly **1,700 acres** of parks and open space, and around **65 parks and playgrounds**, with more athletic complexes being added.

- **Ritter Farm Park:** a dog park, trails and old orchard trees.
- **Casperson Park:** right on Lake Marion.
- Some parks even have bonfire areas, great for get-togethers.

**Applewood Orchard** is a popular fall apple-picking stop.

## Food and drink

Over the last decade, Lakeville has gained a lot of non-chain restaurants:

- **B-52 Burgers** for a great burger
- **Porterhouse** for a special-occasion steak
- **The Chart House**, with a great patio
- **Northern Tap House**
- **The Buckboard** for breakfast (around as long as I can remember)
- **Teresa's** for Mexican
- **Hairy Scratch Kitchen** for something unique

For breweries, try **Lakeville Brewing Company** and **Angry Inch Brewing**.

## Getting around

The **Minnesota Valley Transit Authority (MVTA)** connects Lakeville with Apple Valley, Eagan, Prior Lake, Rosemount, Savage and Burnsville. Heading downtown for a Vikings, Twins or Timberwolves game? The **I-35W E-ZPass express lane (formerly MnPASS)** starts in Lakeville and runs toward downtown Minneapolis. It's free with two or more people in the car or on a motorcycle, and solo drivers can pay a toll to use it.

## Still growing

Lakeville has lots of surrounding land and could someday be among the largest cities in Minnesota. It's close to the Twin Cities, but far enough out that you'll still see cows and cornfields.
