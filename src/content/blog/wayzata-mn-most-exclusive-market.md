**Most expensive neighborhoods just have high prices. Wayzata has something different: almost no inventory, no land left to build on, and a $50 million active listing. Here's why Minnesota's most exclusive market has become so hard to get into, and what buyers are doing to get in anyway.**

Figures are as of my video above (May 2026).

## The numbers

- ZIP code **55391** came in at about **$525 per square foot**, the highest in Minnesota and more than double most Twin Cities suburbs.
- The median listing price was in the **upper $1 millions**, with new listings closer to **$2 million**, and homes selling around **$494 per square foot**.
- Values were up about **5%** year over year.

In Wayzata, the floor is about a million dollars, and there's barely anything available even there.

## Record listings and off-market deals

Many of the biggest Wayzata sales never hit the MLS. They're sold privately, often for cash, with no open houses.

- The **Lake Point estate** on Lake Minnetonka is listed at **$50 million**: nearly 29,000 square feet with guest houses, a pool and a boathouse on about 8 acres and 650 feet of shoreline. It was quietly shopped at $68 million before reaching the MLS, and it's the most expensive listing in Minnesota history.
- An **$8.9 million** Wayzata sale last year (with a basketball court, arcade and pool) was the largest Twin Cities residential sale since 2023.

Luxury buyers are less rate-sensitive, according to Minnesota Realtors' 2025 annual report. Their wealth is often tied to the stock market, and they move when they want to.

## Why Wayzata

**Location.** About 11 miles west of downtown Minneapolis on the north shore of **Lake Minnetonka**: about 14,500 acres and 125 miles of shoreline across dozens of bays. Nobody is building more lakeshore. Minnesota's 17 Fortune 500 headquarters create a steady stream of executives who want lakefront living.

**History.** Wayzata was platted in 1854, the railroad arrived in 1867, and by the 1890s it was a resort for the region's elite. That prestige has been building for 150-plus years.

## The lifestyle

Wayzata has a walkable small-town feel with big-city amenities along **Lake Street** by the water.

- **Dining:** Gianni's Steakhouse (family-owned for 25-plus years and a Wine Spectator award winner every year since 2017), Cov right on the lake, and 6Smith.
- **Shopping:** the Promenade of Wayzata, with boutiques and a lawn for community events.
- **On the water:** the Wayzata Yacht Club, one of the country's best-known sailing clubs, and a marina with about 70 slips and a dock crew in season.
- **Trails:** the paved **Dakota Rail Regional Trail** runs about 17 miles from Wayzata and continues into Carver County, and it connects to the Luce Line State Trail.

**Events:** **James J. Hill Days** every September, Lake Minnetonka's largest festival, drawing 80,000-plus people with music, a craft beer festival, 150-plus vendors and a parade. And the **Chili Open** each February, billed as the world's largest ice golf tournament: four nine-hole courses on frozen Wayzata Bay, 2,000-plus golfers and a chili cook-off. Only in Minnesota.

## Why inventory is so tight

There were only about **35 active single-family listings** in all of Wayzata, and the real number is smaller because so many sales happen off-market.

1. **Owners don't leave.** Families hold these properties for generations. When someone sells, it's usually to downsize or leave the state.
2. **There's no land.** New construction is close to zero.

Homes go pending fast, often against all-cash offers. Metro supply was about 2.4 months, and Wayzata was tighter. Twin Cities luxury sales rose more than 22% in 2025.

## How buyers still get in

- **Condos and townhomes** occasionally come up, some from the high $800,000s to just over $1 million.
- **Fixer-uppers on smaller lots**, still over $1 million, but with real room to add value.
- **Neighboring Lake Minnetonka communities** like [Deephaven](/communities/deephaven), [Orono](/communities/orono) (median listing in the mid-$1 millions) and [Minnetonka Beach](/communities/minnetonka-beach), with similar lake access at a lower entry point.
