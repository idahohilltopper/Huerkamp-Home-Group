**Highview Park is a large new D.R. Horton development in Shakopee, with roads just going in when I filmed. It includes 70 twin-home lots, something I've rarely seen as new construction in more than two decades of selling real estate, plus single-family homes.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (October 2024). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## Why twin homes?

With a **twin home**, you own your lot, not just the unit, so you can **fence your yard** (great for pets). The association handles **mowing and snow removal**, much like a townhome. It's an entry-level price point with more ownership than a typical townhome. [Shakopee](/communities/shakopee) has easy access to Highways 169 and 101; see [why Shakopee will never be the same](/blog/shakopee-mn-growth-amphitheater).

## The Duet (twin home)

- About **1,650 square feet** without a basement, with a two-car garage.
- An open main floor with lots of natural light.
- **Four bedrooms on one level** upstairs (one could be a sitting room), a full bath, laundry and an owner's suite with a 3/4 bath.
- About **$385,000**, plus about $40,000 for a basement option.

## The Princeton (twin home)

- About **2,000 square feet** without a basement.
- An eat-in kitchen with an island for three, a TV area, a pantry and storage.
- Upstairs: a **loft**, an owner's suite with a large walk-in closet, and **four bedrooms on one level**, some with views of a pond with a fountain.
- About **$415,000**, plus about $40,000 for a basement option.

## Single-family plans

- **The Henley:** five bedrooms, three baths and about 2,600 square feet with a main-floor office, smart-home features (even the light switches), a tankless water heater, sod and landscaping, four bedrooms plus a loft upstairs, and an option to finish the basement for a sixth bedroom and fourth bath. Around **$575,000 to $600,000** depending on options (the model's finished basement was extra).
- **A slab-on-grade two-story** with the mechanicals upstairs, an owner's suite, a loft, laundry and four bedrooms, from about **$475,000 to $600,000** depending on a two- or three-car garage and a basement option (roughly $50,000 more).
