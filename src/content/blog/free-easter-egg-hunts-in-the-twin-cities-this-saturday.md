## 🐣 Free Easter Egg Hunts for All Ages in the Twin Cities THIS Saturday

Spring is in full swing, and that means it’s time for one of the most beloved springtime traditions—**Easter egg hunts!** Whether you're looking for **family-friendly fun** or a unique **adult event**, we’ve rounded up some of the best (and free!) options happening across the **Twin Cities metro** on **Saturday, April 19, 2025**.

## 📍 Minneapolis

**Waite Park Egg Hunt**  
🕘 10AM–12PM  
7,000+ eggs hidden and divided by age groups.  
1810 34th Ave NE

**Logan Park Egg Hunt**  
🕘 10AM–12PM  
Games, prizes, and no registration required—just show up and enjoy!  
680 13th Ave NE

**Farview Park Hunt**  
🕐 1PM–3PM  
A fun hunt sponsored by the Twin Cities Bikers Association.  
621 N 29th Ave

## 📍 St. Paul

**Newell Park (Resurrection City)**  
🕘 10AM–11:30AM  
An allergy-friendly hunt with fun for all ages.  
900 Fairview Ave N

**Harmon Park, West St. Paul**  
🕘 10AM  
3,000+ eggs, plus golden egg prize opportunities!  
230 Bernard St. W

**Kaposia Park, South St. Paul**  
🕘 9AM–10:15AM  
For kids ages 1–8—includes candy hunts, a petting zoo, and a visit from the Easter Bunny!  
1028 Wilde Ave

## Adult Easter Egg Hunt

Adults can enjoy the fun too! Here is an **adult-only egg hunt** happening this weekend:

**Hewing Hotel Adult Egg Hunt – Minneapolis**  
🕛 Saturday, April 19 at 11:00 AM  
Join an adults-only rooftop egg hunt with prizes like craft cocktails, hotel perks, and more. One lucky winner will receive an overnight stay at the Hewing Hotel. Must be 21+ to attend.  
300 Washington Ave N  

### **🐰 Pro Tips:**

- Arrive early—many of these events start right on time and the eggs disappear fast!
- Bring your own basket or bag for collecting eggs.
- Some events may be outdoors on grass or woodchips—consider boots or sneakers!
