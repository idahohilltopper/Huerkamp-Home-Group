**The Ranch is an acreage neighborhood in Credit River with home sites around 2.5 acres. In the video above, I toured a Cuddigan Custom Homes executive home: about 5,000 square feet with five bedrooms, five baths and a three-car garage.**

Details are as of my video above (fall 2024). Lot availability changes, so check what's current. For another builder at The Ranch, see [the HighMark rambler tour](/blog/credit-river-the-ranch-highmark).

## The location

[Credit River](/communities/credit-river) is just a few miles west of **I-35**, about 20 minutes from the airport, with Target and Walmart close by. It has a country feel without being remote. When I filmed, about 13 home sites remained in phase one, with about 20 more planned in phase two. More in [where I'd live in 2026](/blog/where-id-live-twin-cities-2026).

## The main floor

- A **living room** with whitewashed oak floors, expansive windows, a detailed ceiling, a subtle arch into the kitchen, and a linear gas fireplace with built-in shelving.
- A **kitchen** with an island farm sink, a **top-loading Fisher & Paykel dishwasher drawer** plus an overflow drawer, a six-burner range and Fisher & Paykel refrigeration, white cabinets and granite.
- A **full butler's pantry** with a second sink and loads of storage.
- A **mudroom** with a second pantry, four lockers and a built-in office nook.
- An east-facing **deck** for sunrises, leading to a **screened porch** with a two-sided fireplace and a second deck, with stairs down to a patio. Decks are maintenance-free, and the yard has in-ground irrigation.
- An **informal dining area** (no formal dining room; most clients tell me they'd only use it three times a year), a second sitting room and a tucked-away powder room.

## Upstairs

- A **loft** with lots of recessed lighting and a **laundry room** with a sink and folding space.
- **Three bedrooms** with walk-in closets: one with a private bath, two sharing a Jack-and-Jill bath with dual vanities and tons of storage.
- An **owner's suite** with a tray ceiling and uplighting, farm-field views and a huge custom closet. The bath has a split vanity, a soaking tub, a two-head shower and a private water closet.

## The lower level

Mirroring the main floor in different colors: a family room with a fireplace, a **wet bar** with matte counters and its own dishwasher, a walk-out patio, a **golf simulator room**, another bedroom, an exercise room, a full bath and **dual-zone heating**.
