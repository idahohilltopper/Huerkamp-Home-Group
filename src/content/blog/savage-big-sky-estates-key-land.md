**Big Sky Estates is a newer community in Savage, mostly two-story homes, with lots still available when I filmed. In the video above, I toured Key Land Homes' Morgan model: five bedrooms, four baths and about 3,700 to 3,800 square feet.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (December 2024). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## The location

[Savage](/communities/savage) is in the south metro with good access to both **Highway 169** and **I-35W**. The airport and either downtown are typically under 30 minutes away. The neighborhood was less than five years old when I filmed. More on Savage's parks and trails in [5 suburbs nobody talks about](/blog/overlooked-twin-cities-suburbs).

## The main floor

- A **large front office** that fits two people working from home, with a slatted accent wall.
- **10-foot ceilings** and 8-foot solid-core doors, which give the whole floor a sense of volume.
- A **living room** with a linear fireplace and a conduit already run for wall-mounted TV cables.
- A **kitchen** in deep navy and white with gold hardware and faucet, quartz counters, a single-basin sink, LG stainless appliances with true double ovens, and a gas cooktop.
- An **eat-in dining area** with lots of windows (formal dining rooms are rare in new builds now) and a patio door to the yard.
- A **butler's pantry** with a beverage fridge, a second sink, a **second dishwasher**, floating shelves and a coffee station, plus a walk-in pantry.
- A **rear staircase** tucked away from the main living space, a big mudroom with a floating bench, and a powder room off the kitchen.

## Upstairs

- A **huge laundry room** with quartz counters, a sink and lots of cabinets.
- **Three bedrooms with walk-in closets**: two share a Jack-and-Jill bath, and one has its own 3/4 bath.
- An **owner's suite** with a vaulted ceiling around 12 feet at the peak, a walk-in closet, a soaking tub, a large glass-enclosed tiled shower and a double vanity.

## The lower level

A **lookout** basement with 9-foot ceilings, a big family room, another bedroom with a walk-in closet, a 3/4 bath and two storage areas. The foundation is poured concrete.

## What it cost

The Morgan model had lots of upgrades, but the plan could be had starting in the **low $800,000s**, which is a lot of home for the money.
