**Bridle Creek is a 95-home new-construction community in Jordan, with 58 lots still available when I filmed. In the video above, I toured two homes that show how much space you can still get for the money.**

Details and pricing are as of my video above (fall 2024). Builder plans and prices change, so check current availability.

## Jordan and the community

[Jordan](/communities/jordan) is in the southwest corner of the metro, with a small-town feel, farm fields minutes away and easy access to **Highway 169** into the Twin Cities. Bridle Creek has a **pool and clubroom** for residents.

Every home includes **sod and an irrigation system**, smart-home controls, a **tankless water heater**, a Bryant furnace and AC, an air exchanger, a sump pump and a **passive radon system** already installed.

## A five-bedroom home for about $467,000

- A welcoming entry into a kitchen with a breakfast bar, stainless appliances, a pantry and a coffee station.
- Luxury vinyl plank floors and a living room with a fireplace.
- A private, east-facing backyard (morning sun) backing up to farm fields.
- A **main-floor bedroom** with a 3/4 bath across the hall.
- Upstairs: a west-facing **owner's suite** with a walk-in closet, double sinks, a walk-in shower and a separate toilet room; a **loft**; three more bedrooms; a full bath; and the laundry.

At almost **2,500 square feet**, five bedrooms and three and a half baths for about **$467,000**, you get a lot for your money in the Twin Cities.

## The Pine plan for about $440,000

- About **2,000 square feet** with **four bedrooms on one level** upstairs and two and a half baths.
- A huge kitchen island with a breakfast bar.
- A loft, upstairs laundry, and an owner's suite with a walk-in closet, double vanity and 3/4 bath.
- An unfinished **lookout basement with egress windows**, roughed in for a bath and another bedroom. It can be finished before closing.
- Room for a deck (not included).
